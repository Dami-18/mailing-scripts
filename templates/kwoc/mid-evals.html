Greetings from Kharagpur Open Source Society,

We hope you are fine and have made some good contributions till now. This email is a gentle reminder about the mid-evaluations. It is compulsory to pass mid-evals, end-evals and submit a blog to be a successful participant in KWoC24.

We hope you have already met the criteria. If not, please try to complete before the deadline. Also, please do not limit yourselves to minimum contribution guidelines. We highly encourage you to contribute as much as possible and learn a lot. We hope KWoC24 helps in enhancing your open-source journey.

For any further queries, feel free to contact us!
