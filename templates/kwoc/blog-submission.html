KWoC 24: Blog Submission

Greetings from Kharagpur Open Source Society,

As we approach the conclusion of Kharagpur Winter of Code 2024, we would like to remind you about the blog submission and the corresponding guidelines to be followed.

The blog can be as descriptive as you want but must contain at least the following points:

  • List of projects you worked on and pull requests you created, along with the links to the PRs
  • A concise yet informative summary of your work and your learnings.

You can publish this in a blog-like format on any platform you choose, like Medium or Github Gist.

Link to the google form for blog submission: KWoC 24 Blog Submission

The deadline for submitting your report is January 23rd. Failing to submit the report by this date will not count towards successful participation.

For any further queries, feel free to contact us!
